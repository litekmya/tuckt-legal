Privacy Policy

Tuckt · Last updated: 20 July 2026

In short: Tuckt does not collect your personal data. Your name, training level, workout history and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

1. Data stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • the name you enter and the training level you choose;
  • your workout history, streaks and progress;
  • your settings: reminder time, language, theme, sound preference.

This information never leaves your device. We have no server and no account system, so we cannot access it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

2. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see or receive your payment details.

To manage subscription status we use RevenueCat, Inc. It receives a purchase identifier and an anonymous device identifier so the app knows whether your subscription is active. It does not receive your name or any data listed in section 1. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

3. Notifications

Reminders are created and delivered locally by your device. We do not send push notifications and do not operate a notification server.

4. Analytics and advertising

Tuckt contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising and no tracking. We do not build user profiles and do not share data with advertisers.

5. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Your rights

Because all data stays on your device, you control it directly: clear it in the app settings or delete the app. There is nothing on our side to request, correct or erase.

7. Health disclaimer

Tuckt provides fitness guidance only and is not medical advice. The stomach vacuum involves breath holding and has contraindications, including pregnancy, the early postpartum period, high blood pressure, heart conditions, hernia and recent abdominal surgery. Consult a doctor before starting if you have any health concerns.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
