Privacy Policy

Tuckt · Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short: Tuckt has no accounts and no server of its own. Your name, training level and workout history stay on your device. We use a few services to run the app: Apple for payments, iCloud and Apple Health; RevenueCat for subscriptions; PostHog for anonymous usage analytics; and, only for users of the Russian App Store, Yandex for optional rewarded ads. We do not sell your data and do not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

1. Data stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, on your iPhone or iPad:

  • the name you enter, your profile photo (if you add one) and the training level you choose;
  • your workout history, streaks and progress;
  • your settings: reminders, language, theme, sound.

We cannot access this information. If you delete the app, it is deleted with it (except the iCloud copy described below).

2. iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps a small copy of your progress in your own iCloud account: your current and best streak, the date of your last workout and your progress in each plan. This lets you continue after reinstalling the app or on a new iPhone. It does not include your name, photo or workout history. This data is stored by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

3. Apple Health (optional)

If you allow it, Tuckt saves your completed practices to Apple Health as workouts. The app does not read any data from Apple Health. Health data is never used for analytics or advertising and is never shared with any third party.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see or receive your payment details.

To check subscription status we use RevenueCat, Inc. It receives purchase information and an anonymous app user identifier generated on your device. It does not receive your name or the data listed in section 1. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. Usage analytics

To understand which parts of the app work and where people get stuck, we use PostHog (servers in the European Union). It receives:

  • an anonymous identifier (the same anonymous identifier RevenueCat uses), not linked to your name or Apple Account;
  • events about using the app, for example "onboarding completed", "subscription screen shown", "practice started" or "practice completed", with the practice and plan day;
  • technical data: device model, iOS version, app version, language, and approximate location (country or city) derived from your IP address;
  • for some sessions, a recording of the app's screens. Text fields, images and your name are masked in these recordings.

PostHog also receives subscription events from RevenueCat (for example "trial started") linked to the same anonymous identifier. This data is used only to improve Tuckt. It is not used for advertising, not sold and not combined with data from other companies. See the PostHog Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising (Russian App Store only)

Users whose App Store account is in Russia usually cannot pay for subscriptions. For them only, Tuckt offers an optional short video ad that opens one premium workout or premium statistics for the day. The ad is shown only when you tap the button to watch it.

Ads are provided by Yandex (Yandex Mobile Ads SDK, which includes AppMetrica). When you watch an ad, Yandex may receive your IP address, device and app information, an identifier of the app on your device (IDFV) and information about the ad shown. Tuckt does not ask for permission to track you, so Yandex does not receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). See the Yandex Privacy Policy.

For users of other App Store countries, the advertising SDK is not started and no data is sent to Yandex.

7. Notifications

Reminders are created and delivered locally by your device. We do not send push notifications and do not operate a notification server.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Your rights

You control the data on your device: change it in the app or delete the app. Analytics data is anonymous and kept for up to one year. If you want it deleted or have any question about your data, write to us at the address below and we will help.

10. Health disclaimer

Tuckt provides fitness guidance only and is not medical advice. The stomach vacuum involves breath holding and has contraindications, including pregnancy, the early postpartum period, high blood pressure, heart conditions, hernia and recent abdominal surgery. Kegel exercises are not a treatment for any condition. Consult a doctor before starting if you have any health concerns.
